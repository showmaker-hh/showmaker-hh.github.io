About me

Understanding how environments shape human wellbeing.

I am a graduate student in Urban Planning at Texas A&M University, with an interdisciplinary background in tourism management and environmental psychology. My research examines the relationship between people and places, particularly the effects of natural environments, urban green space, and leisure experiences on psychological restoration and quality of life.

I use spatial analysis, quantitative methods, and empirical research to study how planning decisions can support healthier, more inclusive communities.
