01 / INTRODUCTION

A little
about me.

I am a Master of Urban Planning student at Texas A&M University, with a background in tourism management from Central South University of Forestry and Technology.

My work examines how natural and built environments influence psychological restoration and subjective wellbeing, and what these relationships mean for planning healthier, more inclusive places.

I combine empirical approaches—including psychophysiological measurements, surveys, GIS, spatial analysis, and computational methods—to connect experiences of places with evidence that can inform planning and management.
